JellyWrist / Wear OS direction board

One product. Four interface temperaments.

Same offline-music flows. Same locked Orbit Pulse identity. Same proposed navigation. The variable is what the watch feels like in the first three seconds.

Grounded in the live repo: 216dp round baseline, Wear Material 3, crown navigation, OLED black, status as sentences, and exactly one gradient control per screen.

All four change together
01 / RecommendedCalm clarity

Orbit Stack

The proposed redesign rendered honestly: one clear status, a short collection stack, and a reliable EdgeButton.

Character
Quiet, native, readable. Brand appears as hierarchy—not purple confetti.
Crown
Predictable vertical list; focused rows scale gently at optical center.
Risk
Least theatrical. It wins by never making the user think.
Why it leadsLowest novelty tax; strongest match to DEC-014.
02 / EmotionalAlbum-first

Cover Stage

Make offline ownership feel tangible: large art, fewer items above the fold, and a player that resembles an object.

Character
Warmest, most recognisably musical; cover quality drives the atmosphere.
Crown
Vertical cover cards snap softly into focus; previous and next peek.
Risk
Low-quality or missing artwork becomes painfully visible.
Choose ifMusic emotion matters more than density.
03 / UtilityStatus-first

Signal Ledger

Treat a self-hosted offline library like a dependable instrument: words, counts, constraints, and almost no decoration.

Character
Precise and calm under failure; the sync state always wins the hierarchy.
Crown
Fastest scan through dense lists; minimal transformations and clean dividers.
Risk
Can feel like an excellent diagnostic tool rather than a music product.
Choose ifSelf-hoster trust beats album-cover theatre.
04 / BoldRound-native

Edge Orbit

Let the hardware shape speak: edge progress, one centered choice, and the Orbit Pulse loop made structural.

Character
Most ownable and watch-specific; the round screen becomes part of the brand.
Crown
Selected row settles at center with clear haptic detents; peers recede.
Risk
Needs ruthless 192dp testing; circular composition leaves little forgiveness.
Choose ifYou want people to recognise JellyWrist at a glance.

What does not change between options
